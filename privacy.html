Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Who we are

Moth Studio is a private desktop application used by a single person, the owner of the YouTube channel Moth's Route, to upload that channel's own videos. It is not available to the public.

YouTube API Services

Moth Studio uses the YouTube API Services. By using Moth Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Moth Studio requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and the synthetic content disclosure to the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's own channel and video details, such as upload status and basic video statistics.

Moth Studio does not access, collect or store information about any other YouTube user, viewer or commenter.

How we use information

Information is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel. Each upload is approved by the owner before it is sent.

How information is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Moth Studio has no server, and no information is sent anywhere except directly to Google and YouTube through the YouTube API Services.

Sharing

Moth Studio does not sell, rent, share or disclose any information to third parties. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Retention and deletion

Stored API data is kept only as long as needed to manage the owner's uploads and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Deleting the Moth Studio app data on the owner's computer removes all stored tokens and data.

How to revoke access

You can revoke Moth Studio's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
