Frag Haul Studio

Frag Haul Studio is a private, single-user desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Frag Haul to upload short vertical clips of gaming streams to that same channel.

All footage comes from paid clipping campaigns or from creators who have approved its use, and every clip credits the original streamer. Every upload is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before it is sent.

Frag Haul Studio uses the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.
