Moth Studio

Moth Studio is a private, single-user desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Moth's Route to upload that channel's own original, AI-assisted videos to that same channel.

Every upload is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before it is sent. Uploaded videos are marked with YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure.

Moth Studio uses the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.
